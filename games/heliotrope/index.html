1. First Light

You are the sun.

Every step you take, every vine in the garden grows one step toward you.
You never touch anything — you only shine. Lead each vine to its ring, and the garden blooms.

Vines float across water as lily pads you can walk on. On land they are hedges in your way — and they love you enough to trap you.

Later: thorn brambles seek you too — they never bloom, they only block, and they doze until you come near. Stone lanterns outshine you: any vine close to one grows toward the lantern instead.
